        About the method

        How LumenX sees in the dark.

        Most enhancers brighten a photo and hope. LumenX measures what the camera actually recorded, restores it with a deep model, repairs what brightening breaks, and shows its working at every step.

        CIDNetHighlight repairGain-aware denoiseSafety audit
        The pipeline

        Five steps from black to light.

        Each image below is the engine's real output at that step, from the recorded run on the landing page.

        1. 01

          Diagnose

          Measures how dark the scene is, how noisy the sensor was, and how much real signal survived, before choosing anything.

        2. 02

          Restore with CIDNet

          A deep model brightens the scene in a colour space built for low light, recovering tone and colour that simple gain would wash out.

        3. 03

          Repair highlights

          Where lamps and windows would blow out, LumenX blends in a protected copy pixel by pixel instead of clipping them to white.

        4. 04

          Clean the grain

          Noise is removed only where brightening amplified it, so texture in well-lit areas is left untouched.

        5. 05

          Audit and report

          Every result is checked for blown highlights, colour shift, lost detail and halos, and each check is reported with its value, pass or flag.

        I

        Measured, not guessed

        Settings are chosen per photo from what the sensor recorded, not from a fixed preset.

        II

        Shown, not hidden

        Every stage, map and rejected setting is visible, so a result can be inspected instead of trusted blindly.

        III

        Honest when unsure

        Unmeasured scores stay empty with a reason, and recoverability is reported as an estimate.
